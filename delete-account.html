TCG Referee

Account Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your TCG Referee account and all associated data at any time. Your account data includes your name, email address, and tournament registration history.

How to Delete Your Account

1
Open the TCG Referee app and go to the Tournament tab.
2
Tap Sign Out to remove your session from the device.
3
Send an email to request full account and data deletion from our servers.
✉ Request Account Deletion
What gets deleted: Your name, email address, and tournament registration history are permanently removed from our servers within 30 days of your request. Data stored locally on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.
