TCG Referee

Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 1, 2026

Overview

TCG Referee ("we," "our," or "the app") is a trading card game judge assistant app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: When you sign in, we collect your name and email address to identify you for tournament registration.
  • Tournament registration: Name, email, and game preference when registering for a tournament event.
  • Feed posts: Any text you post to the tournament community feed.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device identifiers: A random device ID stored locally on your device to track spin-to-win participation. This is not linked to your identity.
  • Game preferences: Your selected game is stored locally on your device to restore your session.
  • Session data: Sign-in information is stored locally on your device using localStorage for convenience.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect payment information — payments are processed directly by Stripe.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

How We Use Your Information

  • To register you for tournaments and track your participation.
  • To display community feed posts.
  • To provide AI-powered card rulings via Google Gemini (your ruling questions are sent to Gemini; we do not store them).
  • To process tournament entry payments via Stripe (we only pass your email to Stripe's payment link).

Third-Party Services

TCG Referee uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini AI — For generating judge rulings. Questions are sent to Google's API. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — For tournament entry fee payments. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • YGOPRODeck API — For Yu-Gi-Oh! card lookup. No personal data is sent.
  • Pokémon TCG API — For Pokémon card lookup. No personal data is sent.
  • Scryfall API — For Magic: The Gathering card lookup. No personal data is sent.
  • Cloudflare Workers — Our backend infrastructure. Your account data is stored in Cloudflare KV storage.
  • Google AdMob — For displaying advertisements in the mobile app. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage & Security

Your account information (name and email) is stored in Cloudflare's KV storage infrastructure. We use industry-standard security practices. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Children's Privacy

TCG Referee is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account data at any time by emailing us. You can sign out of the app at any time, which removes your locally stored session data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page reflects any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
